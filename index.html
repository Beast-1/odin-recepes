[ ABOUT ]

Turning research into things people can actually use.

I like the part of AI/ML that most people skip past — taking an idea that only works in a notebook and turning it into something reliable enough to ship. That's meant building autonomous agents on top of LLMs, an autonomous 3D-content pipeline called ARGUS, and full-stack web apps to put it all in front of real users.

Day to day that's Python on the backend/ML side, React on the frontend, Supabase for the boring-but-critical infrastructure, and Flutter when the product needs to live on a phone.
